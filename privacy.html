Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Satella Utility Bot ("the Bot") may process information when you use its features on Discord.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled, the Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, moderation records such as warnings, and server configuration settings. If welcome messages or moderation logs are enabled, related event information may also be processed.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the Bot's features, including moderation, warnings, server configuration, welcome messages, and moderation logs where enabled.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the Bot's database or hosting environment. Retention depends on the feature and its configuration. Information may remain until it is deleted through an available Bot feature or by the developer.

4. Sharing

The Bot does not need to sell personal information to provide its features. Information may be handled by Discord and the hosting or infrastructure providers required to operate the Bot, subject to their own policies.

5. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

Server administrators can control whether to use the Bot and its optional features. For questions or requests about stored information, contact the Bot developer through the support channel provided for Satella Utility Bot.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
